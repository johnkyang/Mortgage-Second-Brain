# Realtor Referral Framework

**Objective (lever):** Leads + Authority. A repeatable system to earn and grow realtor
referrals — John's top-quality channel.

**Why:** Agents don't refer on rates; they refer the LO who **closes reliably,
communicates proactively, and makes them look like a hero to their client.** This framework
makes John that LO — by system, not luck.

> **Default posture: GIVE FIRST.** Especially with in-demand agents (everyone's chasing
> them), be a referral *source* before you ask — it inverts the dynamic and triggers
> reciprocity. See [[wiki/concepts/give-first-reciprocity]].

## The referral psychology (what agents actually want)
1. **Certainty** — deals close on time; no surprises that blow up their commission.
2. **Communication** — proactive updates so they never look uninformed to their client.
3. **More closed deals** — an LO who can qualify *more* of their buyers (DPA, non-QM,
   lower credit) literally makes them more money.
4. **They look good** — you make the agent the hero, not yourself.

## The partner lifecycle (the system)
```
Identify → Connect → Give value first → First co-deal → Reliability loop → Preferred LO → Co-marketing
```

### 1. Identify (who to pursue)
Target agents whose business fits John's edge:
- Work with **first-time buyers** (need DPA expertise).
- Work **self-employed / investor** clients (need non-QM/DSCR/bank-statement).
- Active in **Santa Clarita / LA**, steady volume, good reputation.
→ Track each as an entity in `/wiki/entities`; tier them **A / B / C** by fit + volume.

### 2. Connect + give value FIRST (before asking for anything)
- Offer co-branded buyer education (DPA / first-time-buyer roadmaps).
- Run a lunch-and-learn on programs that help them close more buyers.
- **Bring them a buyer** from John's content/database (the ultimate door-opener).
- Fast, same-day pre-approvals for their pipeline.

### 3. First co-deal → prove reliability
The first shared deal is the audition. Win it with:
- Proactive status updates (to agent + client) at every milestone.
- Hit every date; flag risks early; never go silent.
- Make the agent look great to their client.

### 4. Reliability loop → preferred LO
Consistency over time = becoming the agent's default. Stay top of mind with a light,
**value-based cadence** (market updates, program news, deal wins) — not "got any referrals?"

### 5. Co-marketing (compliant)
Joint content, events, co-branded materials — structured to comply with RESPA.

## Partner tiering + time allocation
| Tier | Definition | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Active, refers / high potential | Weekly value touch + priority service |
| **B** | Occasional / warming up | Biweekly touch + a value play |
| **C** | Prospect / dormant | Monthly nurture |

## Outreach scripts (starter)
**Cold/warm intro (value-led):**
> "Hi [Agent] — I help a lot of [first-time / self-employed] buyers in [area] get approved
> when other lenders say no (down payment programs, bank-statement loans, etc.). I'd love to
> be a resource for your buyers. Could I send over a one-pager, or grab 15 min this week?"

**Bring-a-buyer (best opener):**
> "[Agent], I've got a pre-approved buyer looking in [area/price]. Are you taking referrals
> right now? Happy to connect you."

## Metrics
- Active A/B/C partners · new partners added/mo · referrals per partner · co-deals closed ·
  conversion of partner referrals.

## Compliance notes (RESPA — important)
- **No kickbacks / things of value for referrals** of settlement-service business
  (RESPA Section 8). This governs LO↔realtor relationships specifically.
- Co-marketing / MSAs must reflect **fair market value** for actual services and be split
  proportionally. `TODO: verify` any arrangement with compliance before executing.
- Keep all marketing claims accurate; no guarantees of approval.
