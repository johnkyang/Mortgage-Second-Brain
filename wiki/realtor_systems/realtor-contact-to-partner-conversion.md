# Realtor Contact → Partner Conversion Engine

**Objective (lever):** Leads. John meets **tons of realtors** through the builder projects.
This is the **system to convert those contacts into ongoing referral partners** — the
operational layer beneath the [[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-partner-acquisition-playbook]].

> Capture (acquisition playbook) gets the contact. **This engine converts it.** A contact
> you met once is worth nothing until a system moves it to "sends me deals."

## The pipeline (5 stages — build in Salesforce/ClickUp)
| Stage | Definition | Goal of this stage |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Met** | Captured at a development (name, brokerage, contact, the deal) | Get them to respond |
| **2. Connected** | Replied / accepted connect | Deliver value, stay memorable |
| **3. Nurturing** | Receiving value, no co-deal yet | Earn a first deal or referral |
| **4. First co-deal** | Working a deal together (the audition) | Win it flawlessly |
| **5. Partner (A/B/C)** | Referring on an ongoing basis | Grow + retain (→ referral framework) |

Tag every contact with `realtor-conversion` + current stage. Anyone non-responsive after
the sequence → **Dormant** (drop to a monthly value-only nurture).

## The conversion sequence (after you meet them)
> Value-first, human, not pitchy. Brackets = personalize. Lead with the deal-advocate edge —
> it's your magnet ([[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]]).

- **T+0 (same/next day) — personal follow-up.**
  > "Hey [Agent], great meeting you at [community] — glad we got [buyer] taken care of. I'd
  > love to be a resource for your other buyers too. Saved your number; here's mine."

- **T+3 days — value drop #1 (what makes you different).**
  > "Quick one: I don't just quote rates — I work with builders to get my buyers price,
  > concessions, and buydowns so deals actually close. Here's a one-pager on the programs I
  > use (DPA, self-employed, investor). Steal it for your buyers anytime."

- **T+1 week — soft connect / invite.**
  Connect on social; invite to a lunch-and-learn or offer a quick coffee. Light, no ask.

- **T+2 weeks — value drop #2 (give, don't take).**
  Default (scalable, for everyone): local market intel, a program update, an invite, or the
  deal-advocate edge on a deal they bring. **A-tier only:** bring them a buyer — the fastest
  trust-builder, but buyer supply is scarce so reserve it for top targets (see
  [[wiki/concepts/give-first-reciprocity]]).

- **T+3–4 weeks — the explicit ask.**
  > "[Agent], I'd love to be a go-to lender for your buyers. Next time you've got someone —
  > even a tough one (self-employed, low down payment) — send them my way and I'll make you
  > look great. Who do you have in the pipeline I could help with?"

- **Ongoing (until first co-deal):** monthly value touch (market/program/win). Then run the
  reliability loop and move them into A/B/C tiering
  ([[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-referral-framework]]).

## Win the audition (Stage 4 → 5)
The first co-deal converts a contact into a partner. Win it with proactive updates, hit
every date, the deal-advocate edge, and making the agent the hero. One flawless deal earns
the second referral.

## Metrics (conversion rates between stages)
- Met → Connected · Connected → First co-deal · First co-deal → Ongoing partner ·
  Partners added / month · referrals per converted partner.
Track these to see where contacts stall and fix that step.
