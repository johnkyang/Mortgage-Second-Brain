# Realtor Partner Acquisition Playbook (Builder Gateway)

**Objective (lever):** Leads + Authority. Solve John's stated biggest challenge —
**not enough realtor partners** — by converting the realtors he meets through his active
builder developments into a **durable, ongoing partner roster.**

> ⏳ **Time-sensitive — ~12–18 month window.** The builder account sells out in ~1–1.5
> years, then that pipeline ends. The window to harvest realtor relationships from it is
> **now.** Every agent who walks a buyer through these developments is a warm partner
> prospect — and John has a captive shot at them via the mandatory pre-approval
> (see [[wiki/builder_systems/preferred-lender-conversion-system]]).

## The strategic logic
1. John is the lender on active developments → realtors bring *their* buyers there.
2. Each of those deals is a **free audition**: he can deliver a hero experience and earn
   trust with a brand-new agent at zero acquisition cost.
3. If captured + nurtured, those agents become an **ongoing** referral source that
   **outlives the builder account** — turning a finite asset into a durable one.

This directly de-risks the concentration problem: the builder channel expires; a realtor
roster + the owned database do not.

## The acquisition funnel
```
Agent brings a buyer to the development
   → Capture the agent (name, brokerage, contact, deal)
   → Deliver a hero experience on THAT deal (fast, communicative, makes them look great)
   → Convert: "I'd love to be your lender beyond this community"
   → Onboard into the Realtor Referral Framework lifecycle (A/B/C)
   → Ongoing partner (survives the builder project selling out)
```

## Step-by-step

### 1. Capture every development agent (don't let them slip)
- Log each agent who brings a buyer: name, brokerage, contact, the deal, date.
- Build a "Development Realtors" list in Salesforce/ClickUp with a `builder-gateway` tag.
- Treat each as an entity in `/wiki/entities` as relationships develop.

### 2. Win the audition deal
- Use the reliability loop from [[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-referral-framework]]:
  proactive updates, hit every date, make the agent the hero to their buyer.
- This is the proof that earns the *next* (non-development) referral.

### 3. The conversion ask (after a great experience)
> "[Agent], loved working with you on [buyer] at [community]. I do a lot more than new
> construction — down payment programs, self-employed/bank-statement, investors. I'd love to
> be a go-to lender for your other buyers too. Can I send a quick one-pager / grab 15 min?"

**★ Lead with the deal-advocate edge:** agents refer the lender who gets *their* buyer a
better deal and makes the transaction close. John helping negotiate builder price/
concessions/buydowns is a powerful magnet — see [[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]].

### 4. Maximize touchpoints via the builder (while it's active)
- Ask the builder for **preferred-lender status** + (if possible) **on-site presence** at
  the developments → more agent introductions per week.
- Offer to co-host buyer events / agent education at the community.

### 5. Convert + diversify
- Move responsive agents into the A/B/C tiering and the ongoing value cadence.
- Goal metric: **# of ongoing realtor partners acquired via the builder gateway.**

## Metrics
- Development agents captured · audition deals won · conversion-to-ongoing-partner rate ·
  ongoing partners acquired/mo · referrals from acquired partners (post-development).
