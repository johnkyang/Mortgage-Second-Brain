# Review Generation System

**Objective (lever):** Trust + Leads. Convert happy closings into **public social proof**
that makes the next referral and content piece convert better.

**Why:** Reviews compound — they make referrals say yes faster, lift content credibility,
and improve discovery. John asks inconsistently; a fixed system fixes that.

## The principle
Ask at the **peak emotional moment** (closing/funding), make it **one tap**, and tell them
**exactly what to write about** (specific prompts beat blank boxes).

## Trigger
Primary: **closing day / funding** (same moment as the referral ask — do both).
Backup: 3–5 days post-close text if not yet left.

## The ask (script)
> "[Name], it was honestly a joy helping you. The biggest thing that helps my business is a
> quick Google review — would you mind sharing a couple sentences about your experience? It
> takes 60 seconds and means the world. Here's the direct link: [LINK]."

**Make it specific (prompt them):**
> "If you're not sure what to say — what was the scariest part going in, and how did it
> actually turn out? That helps the next nervous first-time buyer trust the process."

## Reduce friction to one tap
- Send the **direct review link** (Google/Zillow/etc. — `TODO: list John's platforms`).
- Pre-suggest the angle (down payment fear → solved; self-employed → approved; speed; communication).
- Text the link (don't make them search).

## Turn reviews into assets (close the loop)
- Save every review into `/raw/case_studies` (with permission) → testimonial library.
- Repurpose into content: quote graphics, "client win" stories (anonymized as needed) →
  feeds [[CONTENT_STRATEGY.md]].
- Surface best reviews on pre-approval delivery to build trust earlier in the funnel.
