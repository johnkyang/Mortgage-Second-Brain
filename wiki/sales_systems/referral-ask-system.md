# Referral Ask System

**Objective (lever):** Leads (+ Revenue). Turn referrals from "happens sometimes" into a
**repeatable system with fixed triggers and scripts.**

**Why:** John's best clients are referrals, but he asks inconsistently. A consistent ask
at the right moments is the single fastest way to add qualified lead volume — using clients
he's already earned.

## Core principle
Ask when **trust and emotion are highest** and make it **easy and specific**. "Who do you
know?" gets nothing. A specific, low-friction ask gets names.

## The trigger map (when to ask — make these automatic)
| Trigger | Timing | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-approval win** | When you deliver the pre-approval | Relief + excitement, you just removed fear |
| **Offer accepted** | Same day | Peak excitement |
| **Closing day / funding** | At the keys moment | Maximum gratitude |
| **30-day check-in** | ~1 month after close | Settled in, happy, top of mind |
| **Loan anniversary** | Yearly | Re-open the relationship + refi/HELOC angle |
| **Milestones** | Holidays, home-buying anniversary | Natural touch, not salesy |

## The ask (scripts — conversational, specific)
> Keep human. Brackets = personalize. Pick the moment, then make a **specific** ask.

**At funding / keys (in person or call):**
> "[Name], congrats — you officially own a home. Real quick: my business grows almost
> entirely through people like you. If a friend or coworker ever mentions they're thinking
> about buying — or feel stuck on the down payment — would you point them my way? No
> pressure, just text me their name and I'll take great care of them like I did you."

**Specific-trigger ask (more powerful than generic):**
> "Do you know anyone who's renting right now but would love to own — maybe a coworker who
> thinks they can't afford it? Those are exactly the people I help with down payment programs."

**Soft text follow-up (30 days):**
> "Hey [Name]! How's the new place feeling? 🙌 Quick favor — if anyone you know is even
> *thinking* about buying, send them my way. Happy to give them the same no-pressure game plan."

## Make it easy (reduce friction)
- Send a "forward this" intro message they can copy/paste to a friend.
- Give them your booking link + a one-line value prop.
- Offer a free "home buying game plan" call as the gift you give *their* friend.

## Compliance note (mortgage / RESPA)
- A satisfied **client** referring a friend (no compensation) is fine.
- Do **NOT** pay or give things of value for referrals of settlement-service business in a
  way that violates **RESPA Section 8** (this especially affects realtor/partner referrals).
- Keep any client thank-you gifts nominal and **not contingent** on a transaction.
