# The Builder Deal-Advocate (John's Differentiator)

**One-line definition:** John doesn't just quote a rate — he **helps the sales team
persuade the builder** to accept the buyer's price, concessions, and **buy-down credits**,
getting the buyer the terms they want.

**The mechanism:** the builder trusts John's suggestions, and John holds the buyer's
qualification math — so he builds a credible case for *why the buyer needs these terms to
qualify and buy.* Full method → [[wiki/playbooks/deal-advocate-playbook]].

**Why it matters:** This is a rare, defensible competitive edge. It converts John from a
commodity ("which lender has the lowest rate?") into an **advocate who gets the buyer a
better deal** — something an online/outside lender structurally cannot do. It powers
multiple systems at once.

## Why it's a superpower (it wins on three fronts)
1. **Pre-approval SHARE (vs. co-lender):** sales reps steer buyers to the lender who helps
   them *close* deals. John helping them win builder concessions makes him their favorite —
   the decisive tie-breaker under "buyer's choice" routing.
2. **Retention (pre-approval → funded):** the #1 reason a buyer *won't* shop the loan away —
   "the online lender can't get you the builder's price/concessions/buydown; I can." A
   lowball online rate looks small next to real concessions + a funded buydown.
3. **Realtor acquisition:** agents love a lender who gets *their* buyer a better deal and
   makes the transaction close. This is a magnet for new partners
   ([[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-partner-acquisition-playbook]]).

## How to use it
- **Say it explicitly at pre-approval:** position yourself as the buyer's advocate with the
  builder, not just a lender. (Feeds [[wiki/scripts/why-use-me-vs-shopping]].)
- **Quantify wins:** track concessions + buydown credits secured per deal → proof.
- **Make it content:** "How I got my buyer $X in concessions + a rate buydown the bank
  couldn't" → authority + leads ([[CONTENT_STRATEGY.md]]).
- **Lead the realtor pitch with it:** "I help your buyers get the builder to the price and
  terms they want — and I close on time."

## Example language
> "Here's what makes me different: I don't just hand you a rate. I work with the sales team
> to get the builder to come down on price, add concessions, and fund a rate buydown so your
> payment actually fits. An online lender can't do any of that for you. My job is to get you
> the best *deal*, not just the best *quote*."

## Compliance note
Keep claims truthful and outcome-specific to what was actually achieved; never guarantee a
