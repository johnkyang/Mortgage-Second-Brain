# Persuasion-First Content Frameworks (Brunson × Bernays for Mortgage)

**One-line definition:** The applied playbook for making mortgage content that *moves people
to act* — built on Brunson's story/belief-shifting + Bernays' subconscious-desire techniques
— instead of the generic "education" everyone else posts.

**Why it matters:** Everyone explains loan programs. Almost no one shifts beliefs and stirs
desire. That gap is John's content moat — and he's comfortable on camera to exploit it.

## The shift: from EDUCATION to PERSUASION
| Typical mortgage content | John's persuasion-first content |
|---|---|
| "Here's how an FHA loan works" | "The lie that's keeping you a renter" (break a false belief) |
| Facts → bored scroll | Emotion → story → belief shift → action |
| Neutral, safe, forgettable | A point of view, a villain, a stake |

## The engine — 5 layers (use in every piece)
1. **Hook (pattern interrupt + false belief):** stop the scroll by challenging a belief.
   ("No, you don't need 20% down — and waiting is costing you more than you think.")
2. **Story (Epiphany Bridge — Brunson):** a real, emotional client/origin story that gives
   the viewer the same "aha." Emotion first; logic justifies later.
3. **Reframe (false belief → new belief):** dismantle the limiting belief, install the new
   one. (DPA exists; you qualify; now is a decision, not a wait.)
4. **Desire + proof (Bernays):** tie homeownership to the deeper desire (security, legacy,
   freedom, smart-decision identity) + authority/social proof ("buyers like you are doing
   this," real wins).
5. **CTA (engineered consent):** soft, conversation-starting — make the next step feel like
   their idea ("DM me 'PLAN' and I'll show you your options — no pressure").

## Bernays techniques → mortgage moves
- **Subconscious desire:** sell the feeling (keys in hand, kids in the yard), not the loan.
- **Third-party authority + social proof:** client wins, reviews, "the smart buyers are…".
- **Association:** homeownership = identity/legacy/belonging.
- **Symbols:** the sold sign, the porch, the family — visual shorthand for the dream.

## ⚠️ Compliance guardrail
Persuasion applied to **true** messages only. No guarantees, no fabricated results, no
misleading rate/term claims. Anonymize client stories. See [[CLAUDE.md]] §8.
