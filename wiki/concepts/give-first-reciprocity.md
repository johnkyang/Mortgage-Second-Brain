# Give-First Reciprocity (Winning In-Demand Partners)

**One-line definition:** With high-demand referral partners (top agents everyone is chasing),
you win by **giving business first** — being a referral *source* to them — not by asking for
their referrals like everyone else.

**Why it matters:** Top agents have a line of lenders *asking* for their business. Asking
makes you one of the crowd. **Giving** makes you unforgettable and triggers reciprocity. This
is proven in John's own history: the deals he got from the top agent came *because John first
referred clients TO him.*

## The principle
- **Everyone asks. Almost no one gives.** Be the giver.
- Reciprocity is the strongest, most durable bond — and it inverts the power dynamic (they
  feel they owe you, instead of you chasing them).
- Pair giving with being **uniquely useful** (do something others can't) so you're not just
  generous — you're irreplaceable.

## How John applies it (esp. to the [[wiki/entities/top-realtor-champion]])
1. **Send them buyers** — from content, database, builder traffic. The ultimate door-opener.
2. **Make their deals close** — deal-advocate edge ([[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]])
   so they look like the hero.
3. **Serve buyers they can't** — Chinese-speaking buyers
   ([[wiki/concepts/chinese-language-edge]]) the agent may struggle to serve in-language.
4. **Only then, and lightly, ask** — by which point it's reciprocity, not a cold request.

## Anti-pattern (what NOT to do)
- Leading with "got any referrals for me?" / generic "let's grab coffee" with no value.
- Competing on rate alone with an agent who is courted by everyone.
