# Viral Hook Library (Persuasion-Driven)

The first 1–3 seconds decide everything. These are **belief-shifting / desire-triggering**
hooks — not "here are 3 tips." Personalize brackets. Keep every claim true (compliance).

## 1. False-belief shatter
- "The biggest lie keeping you a renter: that you need 20% down."
- "Everything you've been told about [DPA / reverse mortgages] is wrong."
- "If you think you can't afford a home in [city], watch this."

## 2. Contrarian / polarizing (take a stance)
- "Stop waiting for rates to drop. Here's why that's costing you."
- "Your bank is not your friend. Here's what they don't tell you."
- "Most loan officers won't say this out loud…"

## 3. Curiosity gap / open loop
- "I got my buyer $23,000 in concessions. Here's exactly how." *(use real, verifiable #s)*
- "There's a program almost no one in [city] is using…"
- "The one question that tells me if you'll get approved."

## 4. Fear-reversal
- "The thing scaring you out of buying is the thing costing you the most."
- "Renting feels safe. Let me show you what it's really costing you." *(no guarantees)*

## 5. Story tease (Epiphany Bridge open)
- "A client almost walked away from her dream home — until we found this."
- "He was told 'no' by 3 lenders. Then this happened."

## 6. Callout / identity (Bernays: belonging)
- "First-time buyers in [city] — this is for you."
- "Self-employed and been told you won't qualify? Watch this."
- "中文买家们注意了…" (Chinese-speaking buyers, listen up… — Chinese pillar)

## 7. Authority / insider secret
- "After [N] loans, here's the move that gets buyers the best deal."
- "What I tell my own family about buying right now."

## 8. Social proof (Bernays: herd)
- "Why smart buyers in [city] are doing THIS in 2026." *(defensible)*

## Usage
Pair a hook with a belief target → feed [[wiki/content_systems/script-template]]. Track which
hooks drive DMs; promote winners. ⚠️ Only use real numbers/claims you can substantiate.
