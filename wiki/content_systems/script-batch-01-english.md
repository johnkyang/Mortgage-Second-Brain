# Script Batch 01 — English (Restart Set)

Six ready-to-film scripts (~30–60s each) to batch-record and restart. Personalize
[brackets]; deliver in your own voice. **Compliance:** advocacy/effort only, NO promised
outcomes; any real result = past + anonymized + "every situation is different." See
[[wiki/concepts/persuasion-content-frameworks]] guardrail.

---

## 1. "The 20% down lie" — belief: *I need 20% down*
- **Hook:** "The biggest lie keeping you a renter? That you need 20% down."
- **Story:** "I talk to people every week in [city] who've been saving for years, convinced
  they're not even close — sitting on the sidelines while rent goes up."
- **Reframe:** "Here's the truth: there are down payment assistance programs in California
  built exactly for this. Many buyers qualify with far less down than they think — sometimes
  a fraction of 20%." *(no guarantees — eligibility varies)*
- **Desire:** "Imagine the keys in your hand a lot sooner than 'someday.'"
- **CTA:** "Curious if you'd qualify? DM me 'HOME' and I'll walk you through your options —
  no pressure."

## 2. "Stop waiting for rates" — belief: *I'll wait for rates to drop*
- **Hook:** "Waiting for rates to drop before you buy? That decision might be costing you
  more than you think."
- **Story:** "Everyone's waiting for the same thing. And here's what happens when rates dip —
  the buyers who waited all flood back at once. More competition, more bidding wars, higher
  prices."
- **Reframe:** "You can refinance a rate later. You can't go back and buy at today's price.
  'Marry the house, date the rate.'"
- **Desire:** "The people who win in this market aren't the ones who timed it perfectly —
  they're the ones who got in and built equity."
- **CTA:** "Want me to run your real numbers both ways? DM me 'NUMBERS.'"

## 3. "Is your lender even in the room?" — belief: *all lenders are the same*
- **Hook:** "Most buyers have no idea their lender could be fighting for them — with the
  builder, not just the bank."
- **Story:** "When you buy new construction, there's room to talk price, concessions,
  buydowns. But only if someone's actually advocating for you at the table."
- **Reframe:** "That's how I work — I go to bat for my buyers and build the case with the
  builder's team. I can't promise any specific outcome, every deal is different — but an
  online lender isn't even in the room to try." *(optional: "[anonymized past example] —
  results vary")*
- **Desire:** "You deserve someone in your corner, not just a rate quote."
- **CTA:** "Buying new construction in [area]? DM me 'DEAL' before you talk to the builder's
  lender."

## 4. "Self-employed and told no?" — belief: *I won't qualify*
- **Hook:** "Self-employed and a lender already told you that you won't qualify? They might
  be using the wrong playbook."
- **Story:** "Business owners write off everything — smart for taxes, but it makes your income
  look small to a traditional lender. So they say no."
- **Reframe:** "There are loan programs built for self-employed buyers — bank-statement and
  other options that look at your business differently." *(eligibility varies; not a
  guarantee)*
- **Desire:** "You built the business. You shouldn't be locked out of owning a home for it."
- **CTA:** "Self-employed and want a real answer? DM me 'BIZ.'"

## 5. "Cheapest rate online?" — belief: *lowest rate online is the best deal*
- **Hook:** "Chasing the cheapest rate you saw online? Read the fine print before you do."
- **Story:** "A teaser rate isn't a locked rate. The lowest quote often hides points, fees, or
  a lender who can't actually close on your timeline — and on a new build, a blown close date
  is the real disaster."
- **Reframe:** "I'd rather earn your business than win it with a number that won't hold. Send
  me any quote — I'll break it down honestly, apples to apples."
- **Desire:** "The goal isn't the best quote. It's getting to the keys, on time, with no
  surprises."
- **CTA:** "Got a quote you're comparing? DM me 'COMPARE.'"

## 6. "The bank takes your house?" — belief: *reverse mortgage = lose your home*
- **Hook:** "'A reverse mortgage means the bank takes your house.' That's the myth — here's
  the reality."
- **Story:** "I hear this from seniors and their adult kids all the time. The fear is real,
  but it's built on outdated information."
- **Reframe:** "With a reverse mortgage, you keep the title to your home. It's a financial
  tool some retirees use for security and to stay in the home they love." *(not advice;
  individual situations differ — let's see if it even fits)*
- **Desire:** "Peace of mind in retirement — and dignity in the home you've built a life in."
- **CTA:** "Helping a parent figure this out? DM me 'REVERSE' and I'll explain it simply —
  no pressure."
