# Persuasion Video Script Template (Hook-Story-Offer)

Reusable structure for every video. Fill the brackets. Short-form = compress to ~30–60s;
long-form = expand the story + proof. Keep all claims true (compliance).

## Structure (the 5 beats)
1. **HOOK (0–3s) — pattern interrupt + false belief**
   > [Hook from [[wiki/content_systems/viral-hooks]] — challenge a belief.]

2. **STORY (Epiphany Bridge) — emotional, real**
   > "[Client/origin] was [relatable struggle + the false belief they held]. Then [turning
   > point / what we discovered]." → make the viewer feel the same "aha." Anonymize clients.

3. **REFRAME — false belief → new belief**
   > "Here's what most people get wrong: [old belief]. The truth is [new belief]. Which means
   > [what's now possible for the viewer]."

4. **DESIRE + PROOF (Bernays)**
   > Tie to the deeper want (security/legacy/freedom/smart-decision identity) + authority/
   > social proof: "[real result / review / 'buyers like you are doing this']." (Verifiable.)

5. **CTA — engineered consent (soft)**
   > "If [their situation], DM me '[WORD]' and I'll show you your options — no pressure."
   > (Make the next step feel like their idea.)

## Reminders
- ONE belief target per video. Emotion first, logic second.
- Open a loop early, close it late. Cut filler — earn every second.
- ⚠️ True claims only; no guarantees; anonymize clients. See [[CLAUDE.md]] §8.
