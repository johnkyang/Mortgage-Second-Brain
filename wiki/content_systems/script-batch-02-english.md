# Script Batch 02 — English (25 Belief-Shift Hooks)

25 ready-to-film scripts (~30–60s) from John's hook list. Each follows
Hook → Story → Reframe → Desire → CTA. Personalize [brackets]. Also loaded as
individual tasks in the ClickUp Content Pipeline (each task = one video).
**Compliance:** advocacy/effort only, NO promised outcomes; real results = past, anonymized,
"every situation is different." See [[wiki/concepts/persuasion-content-frameworks]].

---

1. **Waiting for rates to drop could cost you more than buying now** — *belief: wait for rates* · CTA: RATES
2. **The wealthy don't avoid debt — they use the right kind of debt** — *belief: all debt is bad* · CTA: LEVERAGE
3. **Your first home doesn't need to be your dream home** — *belief: first home must be perfect* · CTA: START
4. **Renting isn't always cheaper — it just feels safer** — *belief: renting is cheaper/safer* · CTA: RENT
5. **A low rate doesn't always mean the best mortgage** — *belief: lowest rate = best* · CTA: COMPARE
6. **Your monthly payment matters more than the home price** — *belief: focus on price* · CTA: PAYMENT
7. **Down payment assistance is not only for broke buyers** — *belief: DPA = for the poor* · CTA: DPA
8. **Good credit doesn't automatically mean mortgage approval** — *belief: good credit = approved* · CTA: APPROVE
9. **Pre-approval is not about shopping — it's about strategy** — *belief: pre-approval = shopping* · CTA: STRATEGY
10. **Buying with less down doesn't mean a bad decision** — *belief: low down = reckless* · CTA: LESSDOWN
11. **The biggest risk is not buying too early — it's waiting too long** — *belief: waiting is safe* · CTA: WAIT
12. **A mortgage is not just debt — it can be a wealth-building tool** — *belief: mortgage = burden* · CTA: WEALTH
13. **Parents helping you buy may beat waiting for inheritance** — *belief: wait for inheritance* · CTA: GIFT
14. **Reverse mortgages don't mean the bank owns your home** — *belief: reverse = lose home* · CTA: REVERSE
15. **Being house rich and cash poor is not a retirement plan** — *belief: paid-off house = secure* · CTA: RETIRE
16. **Paying off your mortgage early may not be the smartest move** — *belief: pay off ASAP* · CTA: PAYOFF
17. **A 2/1 buydown is not a gimmick when used correctly** — *belief: buydown = trick* · CTA: BUYDOWN
18. **Builder incentives are not free money — but they can be powerful** — *belief: incentives = scam/gift* · CTA: INCENTIVE
19. **You don't need perfect finances to start preparing to buy** — *belief: must be perfect first* · CTA: PREP
20. **The cheapest lender may become the most expensive mistake** — *belief: cheapest = best* · CTA: CHEAPEST
21. **Your realtor and lender should be planning your offer together** — *belief: separate errands* · CTA: TEAM
22. **Most buyers lose homes before they ever write the offer** — *belief: won in negotiation* · CTA: OFFER
23. **Mortgage approval is not about income — it's about documented income** — *belief: high income = approved* · CTA: DOCS
24. **Self-employed buyers are not unqualified — just under-documented** — *belief: self-employed can't qualify* · CTA: BIZ
25. **Real estate is not about timing the market — it's about time in the market** — *belief: time the market* · CTA: TIME

> Full 5-beat scripts for each live in the ClickUp Content Pipeline task of the same name.
