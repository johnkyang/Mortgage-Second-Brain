# Builder Preferred-Lender Conversion System

**Objective (lever):** Revenue + Leads. Maximize the value of John's finite builder
account by winning more of the captive pre-approval flow and converting it into funded
loans — while it lasts.

## The structural reality
- John is **1 of 2 preferred lenders on site.**
- **Every buyer must be pre-approved by one of the two** → captive top-of-funnel.
- Buyers are **not required to use** a preferred lender for the loan → they can shop.

This creates **three levers** — two leakage points and one harvest:
1. **Pre-approval SHARE** — win more of the buyers vs. the other preferred lender.
2. **Pre-approval → FUNDED conversion** — keep the buyers you pre-approve.
3. **Realtor HARVEST** — turn each buyer's agent into an ongoing partner
   (see [[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-partner-acquisition-playbook]]).

---

## Lever 1 — Win more pre-approval SHARE  *(routing = BUYER'S CHOICE, confirmed 2026-05-24)*
Buyers freely pick which preferred lender → share is won by **visibility, responsiveness,
reputation, and program breadth on-site.** No assignment to rely on.
- **Be the obvious, easy choice:** fastest response, instant/on-site pre-approvals, zero
  friction. Speed wins first contact.
- **Win the sales reps anyway:** even with buyer's choice, reps informally influence ("both
  are great, but John is incredibly responsive"). Make every rep's deals smooth → they'll
  nudge buyers your way. They're not gatekeepers, but they're tie-breakers.
- **★ Be the deal-advocate (John's edge):** John helps the sales team persuade the builder
  on price, concessions, and buy-down credits — so he helps reps *close*. This is the
  decisive reason reps steer buyers to him. See [[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]].
- **Differentiate on capability:** you approve buyers the other lender may not (DPA, non-QM,
  self-employed). Become the "go-to for the tougher scenarios" → reps + buyers gravitate to you.
- **Presence + brand on-site:** be visibly present, helpful, and known at the community.
- Track share: **# pre-approvals to John vs. co-lender.** Grow it.

## Lever 2 — Convert pre-approval → FUNDED  *(NO builder incentive, confirmed 2026-05-24)*
⚠️ **No structural lock-in.** Buyers can use any lender with zero penalty → this is the
**highest-risk leak.** Retention rides entirely on relationship, speed, trust, and price.
A pre-approval is a foot in the door, **not a closed loan.** Treat every one as a sale.
- **★ Lead with the deal-advocate edge (strongest retention argument):** "An outside lender
  can't get you the builder's price, concessions, or buydown — I can." Real concessions +
  a funded buydown dwarf a small online rate difference. See
  [[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]].
- **Win the relationship at pre-approval** — consultative discovery ([[SALES_SYSTEM.md]]) so
  they don't *want* to shop. The pre-approval experience IS the sale.
- **Anti-shopping value narrative** — proactively frame why staying with you protects them
  (on-time close, certainty, no bait-and-switch). See
  [[wiki/scripts/why-use-me-vs-shopping]].
- **Speed + certainty** protect the builder's close date and the buyer's deal — remove any
  reason to switch.
- **Be price-aware:** with no incentive, an outside lowball rate is a real threat. Know your
  competitiveness; win on total cost + service, and address rate quotes head-on (not defensively).
- **Follow-up cadence** through the shopping window so a competitor doesn't fill the silence.
- Track: **pre-approval → funded conversion rate** (the #1 KPI for this account).

> **Key efficiency:** the *same behavior* — a fast, communicative, high-trust pre-approval
> experience — wins BOTH Lever 1 (share) and Lever 2 (retention). It's one investment, two payoffs.

## Lever 3 — Harvest the agent
Every buyer comes with an agent. Capture + audition + convert each one into a durable
partner before the projects sell out →
