# Builder Preferred-Lender Conversion System

**Objective (lever):** Revenue + Leads. Maximize the value of John's finite builder
account by winning more of the captive pre-approval flow and converting it into funded
loans — while it lasts.

## The structural reality
- John is **1 of 2 preferred lenders on site.**
- **Every buyer must be pre-approved by one of the two** → captive top-of-funnel.
- Buyers are **not required to use** a preferred lender for the loan → they can shop.

This creates **three levers** — two leakage points and one harvest:
1. **Pre-approval SHARE** — win more of the buyers vs. the other preferred lender.
2. **Pre-approval → FUNDED conversion** — keep the buyers you pre-approve.
3. **Realtor HARVEST** — turn each buyer's agent into an ongoing partner
   (see [[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-partner-acquisition-playbook]]).

---

## Lever 1 — Win more pre-approval SHARE
**Question to resolve:** how is a buyer routed to one preferred lender vs. the other —
**builder sales-rep recommendation, buyer's choice, or rotation?** `TODO: confirm with John.`

If sales reps influence it (usually they do), **the on-site sales team is the gatekeeper.**
- Build real relationships with each on-site sales rep — they steer buyers to the lender who
  makes *their* deals close (the builder is paid when homes close).
- Be the easy choice: fastest pre-approvals, on-site/instant availability, zero friction,
  proactive communication that makes the rep look good to the buyer.
- Give the reps tools: simple one-pager, quick-apply link, "send me the buyer, I'll handle it."
- Track share: **# pre-approvals assigned to John vs. co-lender.** Aim to grow it.

## Lever 2 — Convert pre-approval → FUNDED (stop the shopping leak)
A pre-approval is a foot in the door, **not a closed loan.** Treat every one as a sale.
- **Build the relationship during pre-approval** — consultative discovery
  ([[SALES_SYSTEM.md]]), so they don't *want* to shop.
- **Builder incentive leverage:** many builders give a buyer credit (e.g., toward closing
  costs) for using a preferred lender. If this builder does, it's the single strongest
  retention lever — make sure every buyer knows what they'd *lose* by shopping away.
  `TODO: confirm whether this builder offers a preferred-lender incentive.`
- **Speed + certainty** protect the builder's close date and the buyer's deal — reduce any
  reason to switch.
- **Follow-up cadence** through the buyer's shopping window so a competitor doesn't fill the
  silence.
- Track: **pre-approval → funded conversion rate.**

## Lever 3 — Harvest the agent
Every buyer comes with an agent. Capture + audition + convert each one into a durable
partner before the projects sell out →
