# Realtor One-Pager — "Why Work With Me"

Leave-behind / DM asset for agents. **Positioning:** *"I close your deals and serve buyers
others can't"* — NOT "I'll feed you leads." Copy below is the draft; hand to design (Canva).

> ⚠️ Compliance before publishing: no guarantees of approval/rate/savings; advocacy/effort

## DRAFT COPY

**[Headline]**
> Your buyers deserve a lender who's in their corner — and makes you look like the hero.

**Who I help your buyers buy:**
- First-time buyers (down payment assistance specialist)
- Self-employed / business owners (bank-statement & non-QM)
- Investors (DSCR, non-QM)
- Chinese-speaking buyers — served **in-language** (中文服务)
- New-construction buyers
- Seniors / families (reverse mortgage)

**What makes me different:**
- **I advocate, not just quote.** On new construction I work with the builder's team on price,
  concessions, and buydowns for your buyer. *(Every deal is different — no outcome is
  guaranteed, but I'm in the room fighting for it.)*
- **I get the "hard" ones done.** Self-employed, lower credit, complex income — I have programs
  most lenders don't reach for.
- **I speak your buyers' language.** Literally — Chinese-speaking buyers get a smoother,
  higher-trust experience.

**How I make YOU look good:**
- Proactive updates at every milestone — you're never the last to know.
- Fast pre-approvals; I protect your close date.
- Clear communication with your client so the deal feels easy.

**Let's work together.**
> [Name], Mortgage Loan Consultant · [phone] · [email] · [booking link]
