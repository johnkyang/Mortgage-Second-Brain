# Russell Brunson (Role Model)

**What it is:** Founder of ClickFunnels; teaches story-driven persuasion + funnels
(DotCom Secrets, Expert Secrets, Traffic Secrets).

**Why it matters:** His frameworks turn dry expertise into belief-shifting, action-driving
content — exactly what John wants instead of generic mortgage education.

## Key frameworks to apply
- **Hook → Story → Offer (HSO):** grab attention → tell a belief-shifting story → make an
  ask. The backbone of every piece.
- **The Epiphany Bridge:** tell the origin/turning-point story that made YOU believe, so the
  audience has the same "aha" (emotion sells, then logic justifies).
- **False Belief → New Belief:** identify the limiting belief blocking action and break it
  (vehicle, internal, external beliefs). For mortgage: "I need 20% down," "rates too high,
  wait," "I won't qualify."
- **The Attractive Character:** a relatable persona — backstory, flaws, polarity, point of
  view. People follow a person, not a logo.
- **Curiosity / open loops:** tease, don't dump. Make them stay/return.
- **Dream 100:** go where your ideal audience already gathers (agents, communities, groups).
