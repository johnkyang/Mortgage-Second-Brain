# Edward Bernays (Role Model)

**What it is:** "Father of public relations." His book *Propaganda* (1928) showed how to
shape mass behavior by appealing to subconscious desire rather than logic.

**Why it matters:** His techniques make a message *feel* compelling at a gut level — the
psychological layer most mortgage content completely misses.

## Key techniques to apply
- **Appeal to subconscious desire/emotion**, not facts: people don't want a loan — they want
  security, status, freedom, a home for their kids, a smart-decision identity.
- **Engineering consent:** lead people to a conclusion so it feels like *their* idea (ask
  questions, reframe) rather than a pitch.
- **Third-party authority:** experts, leaders, and "people like them" endorse — more
  persuasive than self-claims.
- **Association:** link the product to an aspirational identity/value (homeownership =
  legacy, belonging, the American dream).
- **Herd instinct / social proof:** "everyone like you is doing this."
- **Symbols & framing:** the keys, the sold sign, the family on the porch.

## ⚠️ Ethics + compliance guardrail (non-negotiable)
Use these to make **TRUE** messages resonate — never to deceive. Mortgage marketing is
regulated: no misleading claims, no guarantees, no manufactured falsehoods. Persuasion of
emotion is fine; manipulation via untruth is not (and is illegal here). See [[CLAUDE.md]] §8.
