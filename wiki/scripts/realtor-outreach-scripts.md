# Realtor Outreach & Conversion Scripts

Copy-paste-ready, personalize [brackets]. Posture: **give-first, lead with the edges (close
their deals + serve buyers they can't), don't promise outcomes.** Pair with the pipeline in
[[wiki/realtor_systems/realtor-contact-to-partner-conversion]].

> ⚠️ Compliance: no guarantees of approval/rate/savings; no things of value for referrals
> (RESPA §8). Advocacy/effort language only. Include NMLS # in written marketing.

---

## 1. Development-floor capture (in person — agent brings a buyer)
> "Hi, I'm John — one of the preferred lenders here at [community]. I'll take great care of
> [buyer]. Are you their agent? Let's connect so I can keep you posted every step — what's the
> best number for you?"
- Goal: get name + number + brokerage. Log immediately (Stage: **Met**).

## 2. Same-day follow-up (text)
> "Great meeting you at [community], [Agent]! I've got [buyer] handled and I'll keep you in
> the loop. Saved your number — here's mine. I'd love to be a resource for your other buyers
> too."

## 3. Value drop #1 — what makes me different (T+3 days)
> "Quick one, [Agent]: I don't just quote rates — I advocate for my buyers with the builder on
> price, concessions, and buydowns, and I work bank-statement / DPA / non-QM scenarios most
> lenders pass on. I also serve Chinese-speaking buyers in-language. Here's a one-pager —
> steal it for your buyers anytime. [link]"

## 4. Value drop #2 — give, don't take (T+2 weeks)
> Default (everyone): "Saw this [market stat / program update] and thought of your buyers in
> [area] — [insight]."
> A-tier only (reserve scarce buyer-referrals): "I've got a pre-approved buyer looking in
> [area/price] — are you taking referrals right now? Happy to send them your way."

## 5. The conversion ask (after a great shared experience, T+3–4 wks)
> "[Agent], loved working with you on [buyer]. I'd love to be a go-to lender for your buyers —
> even the tough ones (self-employed, low down payment, Chinese-speaking). Next time you've got
> someone, send them my way and I'll make you look great. Who's in your pipeline I could help
> with?"

## 6. A-tier give-first (the top agent / [[wiki/entities/top-realtor-champion]])
> "[Agent], I appreciate you bringing me onto [project]. I want to return the favor — I've got
> [a buyer / buyers] I'd like to send your way. Who should I connect them with on your team?"
- Lead with giving (you referred him before; that's why he reciprocated). Don't ask first.

## 7. Lunch-and-learn / education invite
> "I'm doing a quick 20-min session for agents on [DPA programs / getting self-employed buyers
> approved / new-construction financing] — stuff that helps you close more buyers. Want in?
> I'll bring lunch."

## 8. Re-engage a dormant agent
> "[Agent], it's been a minute! I've added some tools that help me get more of your buyers to
> the finish line — [DPA / bank-statement / new-construction advocacy]. If you've got anyone
> stuck or hard to qualify, send them my way. No pressure — just want to be useful."

---

## Usage
- One ask per message; keep it human; track replies in the pipeline.
- Promote whichever scripts get replies; retire what doesn't.
- For written/marketing pieces, add NMLS # + Equal Housing. See [[CLAUDE.md]] §8.
