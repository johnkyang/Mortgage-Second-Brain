# Lead Strategy — Scale the Referral Engine First

**The insight (from interview, 2026-05-24):**
John's **#1 bottleneck is lead volume**, yet his **best clients come from referrals**
(realtor/partner referrals + past clients). These two facts point to one conclusion.

## The strategic conclusion
Don't chase volume in a cold channel (e.g. paid ads) while the channel that already
produces your *highest-quality* clients is under-systematized. **The fastest, highest-ROI
path to more qualified leads is to turn referrals from "happens to me" into a
"system I run."**

Cold channels (ads) add volume but lower average quality and need new skills/spend.
Referral channels are already proven on quality — they just lack a *volume system*.

## The play (priority order)
1. **Past-client / database reactivation + referral engine** (fastest — you already own it)
   - Systematic referral asks at funding + milestones.
   - Review-generation system (social proof → more inbound).
   - Stay-in-touch cadence (value, not "any referrals?").
   - Reactivation campaign to the existing database.
2. **Realtor/partner referral system** (compounding — see [[REALTOR_STRATEGY.md]])
   - Value-first plays that make partners refer more, more often.
   - Make John more *referable* (fast, communicative, makes the agent look great).
3. **Content as the amplifier** (see [[CONTENT_STRATEGY.md]])
   - Content doesn't replace referrals here — it *feeds* them: co-marketing with
     partners, social proof from wins, and "stay top of mind" so referrals remember you.
   - Build a second inbound channel over time (DPA / first-time-buyer lead magnets).

## Why this beats "just run ads"
- Protects average client quality while increasing volume.
- Uses assets John already has (relationships, past clients, reputation).
- Lower cost, faster payback, compounds.

## Next intelligence needed
- Size + state of the past-client database; current referral/review behavior.
- Top realtor partners and *why* they refer (→ [[REALTOR_STRATEGY.md]]).
