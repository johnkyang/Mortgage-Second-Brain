## The partner thesis
A great LO partner makes the realtor's deals **close faster, with fewer surprises, and
makes the realtor look like a hero**. Be the LO who never lets a deal blow up silently.

## Partner lifecycle
```
Identify → Connect → Provide value first → First co-deal → Reliability → Preferred LO → Co-marketing
```

## Value-first plays (give before you ask)
- Co-branded buyer education (DPA, first-time-buyer roadmaps).
- Fast pre-approvals + proactive deal updates that make the agent look great.
- Bring *them* buyers from your content/database.
- Lunch-and-learns on DPA / non-QM / reverse so they can serve more clients.
