# BUILDER_STRATEGY.md — Builder Relationships & New Construction

> Goal: become a preferred/embedded lender for builders so their buyer traffic flows to
> John. Builder deals = volume + predictability when the relationship is real.

## Why builders matter
A single builder relationship can produce a **steady pipeline** of pre-qualified buyers
walking into model homes. The LO who can finance more buyer types (DPA, non-QM, lower
credit) helps the builder **convert more foot traffic into sales**.

## How builders choose lenders
- Reliable closings on contract timelines (builders hate fallout).
- On-site/responsive support for walk-in buyers.
- Ability to qualify a *wider* range of buyers (more sales for them).
- Incentive structures + forward commitments. _TODO: confirm John's capabilities._

## Builder lifecycle
```
Identify active projects → Meet sales team → Pilot buyers → Reliability → Preferred lender → On-site presence
```

## Differentiators to lean on
- DPA expertise → unlocks buyers who think they can't afford new construction.
- Non-QM / bank statement → self-employed buyers in new communities.
- Fast, communicative process → protects builder's close dates.

## Track each builder/project
Entity per builder + per project in `/wiki/entities`; raw notes in
`/raw/builder_projects`:
- Builder, community/project, location, price points, sales team contacts, incentives,
  status, buyers referred.
