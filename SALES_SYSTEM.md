# SALES_SYSTEM.md — Appointment → Conversion → Close

> Goal: convert more conversations into applications and funded loans, with a repeatable,
> emotionally intelligent process. Inspired by consultative selling (e.g. Jeremy Miner /
> NEPQ-style questioning) — to be adapted to mortgage. See `/wiki/role_models`.

## The funnel
```
Conversation → Discovery call → Pre-approval / application → Processing → Funded → Referral
```

## Stage 1 — Discovery (the most important call)
Lead with **questions, not pitches**. Diagnose before prescribing.
- Situation: where are they now (renting, timeline, family, goals)?
- Problem: what's blocking them (down payment, credit, income docs, fear)?
- Consequence: what happens if they don't act?
- Vision: what does success look like for them?

→ Capture real language for `/raw/objections` and `/raw/client_scenarios`.

## Stage 2 — Objection handling
Build a living objection bank in [[wiki/sales_systems/buyer-objections]] _(to build)_.

- "I'm self-employed, I won't qualify." → bank statement / non-QM
- "I don't trust the process / been burned before." → trust + transparency

## Stage 3 — Conversion mechanics
- Clear next step every time (no dead ends).
- Pre-approval as the natural close, not a hard ask.
- Set expectations for the journey (reduces drop-off + anxiety).

## Stage 4 — Follow-up
Speed + persistence. Build sequences in `/wiki/sales_systems/follow-up-sequences`.
Most deals are lost to silence, not "no."

## Stage 5 — Post-close → referral engine
Turn every funded client into reviews + referrals + a content story (anonymized).
