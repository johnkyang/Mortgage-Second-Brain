## How to read this map

- **Sources** answer *"where did this come from?"*
- **Entities** answer *"who/what is involved?"*
- **Concepts** answer *"what's the reusable idea?"*
- **Strategies/Systems** answer *"how do we turn it into revenue?"*

When a new asset is created, add a row/bullet under the right section with an Obsidian
link `[[asset]]` and a one-line description.
