## The 3 inbound engines
1. **Authority content** → DMs/comments → conversation (CONTENT_STRATEGY).
2. **Partner referrals** → warm intro → appointment (REALTOR + BUILDER strategy).
3. **Education funnels** → DPA / first-time-buyer / reverse lead magnets → opt-in → nurture.
