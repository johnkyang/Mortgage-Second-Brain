# CONTENT_STRATEGY.md — Viral & Authority Content Engine

> Goal: turn John into the **go-to mortgage voice in Santa Clarita / LA**, generating
> inbound conversations. Content must build trust, create curiosity, and drive action.

## Operating principle
Content is not for likes. It exists to start **qualified conversations** and build
**authority**. Every piece should map to a buyer fear, an objection, or a "I didn't
know that was possible" moment.

## Audience segments
- **First-time buyers** (fear, confusion, "can I even afford this?")
- **Move-up / repeat buyers**
- **Reverse mortgage prospects** (seniors + their adult children)
- **Investors** (DSCR, non-QM, bank statement)
- **Realtors & builders** (B2B authority + co-marketing)

## Core content pillars
0. **Deal-advocate proof** — "how I got my buyer the builder's price + concessions + a rate
   buydown the bank couldn't" → John's #1 differentiator as content
   (see [[wiki/concepts/builder-deal-advocate]]). High authority + trust.
1. **Affordability & DPA** — "you may need less than you think" (see [[DPA_STRATEGY.md]]).
2. **First-time buyer education** — demystify the process, reduce fear.
3. **Reverse mortgage truth-telling** — bust myths (see [[REVERSE_MORTGAGE_STRATEGY.md]]).
4. **Creative financing / Non-QM** — DSCR, bank statement, no-income loans.
5. **Local market intelligence** — Santa Clarita / LA specifics.
6. **Behind-the-scenes / client wins** — proof + trust (anonymized).

## Hook library → [[wiki/content_systems/viral-hooks]] _(to be built)_
Patterns to mine from transcripts/role models:
- Curiosity gap ("Most buyers have no idea this program exists…")
- Myth-bust ("No, you don't need 20% down.")
- Fear-reversal ("The thing scaring you out of buying is costing you more.")
- Story open ("A client almost walked away from her dream home until…")
- Contrarian ("Everyone says wait for rates to drop. Here's why that's a trap.")
