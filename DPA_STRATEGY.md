# DPA_STRATEGY.md — California Down Payment Assistance

> Goal: own the "you can afford a home sooner than you think" message in CA. DPA is the
> single biggest unlock for first-time buyers who self-disqualify over the down payment.

## Why DPA is a superpower
The #1 reason first-time buyers don't act is the belief that they need 20% down. DPA
programs collapse that barrier. Being the **DPA expert** turns "I can't afford it" into
"let's get you pre-approved."
