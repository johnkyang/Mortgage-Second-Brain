# REVERSE_MORTGAGE_STRATEGY.md — Reverse Mortgage Positioning

> Goal: become the trusted, myth-busting reverse mortgage educator for seniors (62+) and
> their adult children in the LA market. Reverse is an **emotional, trust-first** sale.

## The emotional landscape
Reverse mortgages carry baggage and fear ("the bank takes my house"). The buyer is often
TWO people: the **senior** (wants security/dignity) and their **adult child** (wants to
protect the parent + the inheritance). Win both.

## Positioning
Lead with **education + trust**, not product. Reframe from "debt" to "a financial tool
for a secure retirement / aging in place." Address fear before features.
